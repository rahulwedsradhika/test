## 4. Chapterwise Curriculum Breakdown

The platform structures each course into a rigorous modular syllabus:

```
AIAPGET Master Curriculum
│
├── 1. Samhita Vibhaga (Classical Texts)
│   ├── Charaka Samhita (120 Chapters: Sutra, Nidana, Vimana, Sharira, Indriya, Chikitsa, Kalpa, Siddhi)
│   ├── Sushruta Samhita (186 Chapters: Sutra, Nidana, Sharira, Chikitsa, Kalpa, Uttaratantra)
│   ├── Ashtanga Hridaya (120 Chapters: Sutra, Sharira, Nidana, Chikitsa, Kalpa, Uttarasthana)
│   └── Laghuttrayi (Madhava Nidana, Sharangadhara Samhita, Bhavaprakasha)
│
├── 2. Ayurveda Dravyaguna & Rasashastra
│   ├── Dravyaguna Vijnana: Rasapanchaka, 300+ Dravyas, Karma, Rogaghnata
│   ├── Rasa Shastra: Maharasa, Uparasa, Sadharanarasa, Dhatus, Ratnas, Bhasma Kalpana
│   └── Bhaishajya Kalpana: Panchavidha Kashaya Kalpana, Asava-Arishta, Taila-Ghrita, Avaleha
│
├── 3. Clinical Disciplines (Roga Vibhaga)
│   ├── Kayachikitsa: Jwara, Atisara, Grahani, Rajayakshma, Prameha, Vatavyadhi
│   ├── Shalya Tantra: Shalya Aharana, Yantra-Shastra, Ksharasutra, Agnikarma, Raktamokshana
│   ├── Shalakya Tantra: Netraroga, Karnaroga, Nasaroga, Mukharoga, Shiroroga
│   ├── Prasuti Tantra & Stri Roga: Garbhadhana, Garbhini Paricharya, Asadharana Roga
│   └── Kaumarbhritya: Navajata Shishu Paricharya, Balagraha, Vyadhikshamatva
│
└── 4. Modern Medicine Integration
    ├── Human Anatomy & Physiology High-Yield Tables
    ├── General Pathology, Hematology & Biochemistry Values
    ├── Pharmacology (Modern Antibiotics, NSAIDs, Cardiovascular Drugs)
    └── Forensic Medicine & Medical Jurisprudence
```
