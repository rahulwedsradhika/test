## 5. Question Discrepancy Reporting & Academic Resolution

AyurQuest features an active quality assurance community with over **41,864 reported questions** resolved by faculty:

### Exemplary Verified Case (Screenshot 192641.png)
- **Question:** `मांसगत शल्य को निकालने की विधि है` (Method for extracting a foreign body lodged in muscle tissue)
- **Options:** (A) आहरण (B) भेदन (C) छेदन (D) पाटन
- **Verified Correct Answer:** **(B) भेदन**
- **Classical Citation:** *Sushruta Samhita Sutrasthana 27/5*
- **Student Query (Neha Kudale):** "Sir please explain why Bhedana is correct here instead of Aharana?"
- **AyurQuest Faculty Resolution (Dr. Vandana):** "According to Sushruta Samhita Sutrasthana Chapter 27 (Shalyapanayaniya Adhyaya), shloka 5: *'तत्र मांसगते भेदनं...'* When a foreign body is lodged deep within muscular tissue, an incision (Bhedana) is mandatory to expose and safely extract the shalya. Aharana is applied only when the shalya is superficial, visible and graspable. Hence Option (B) भेदन is authentic."
